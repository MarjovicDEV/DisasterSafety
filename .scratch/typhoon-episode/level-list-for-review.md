# Typhoon Episode: Level list for review

Date: 2026-11-27. Please check each Safe Choice, each Unsafe Choice and its reason, and the sources. The sources are the findings TY-01 to TY-65 in the Typhoon safety research (PAGASA, DepEd, DILG, UNICEF Philippines, NOAA, Ready.gov and the American Red Cross).

How a Level works: the Learner reads the situation and picks one Choice. The Learner's Student acts it out. A Safe Choice plays to the end. An Unsafe Choice stops partway, before anything happens, and the Student says "Hmm, wait..."; then a short, kind explanation shows and the Learner chooses again. Nobody is hurt or shown in danger. Easy Levels have 2 Choices, Normal 3, Hard 4.

Rules used where the sources disagree (full list in the decision record for Typhoon safety rules):

- Classes follow DepEd Order No. 22, s. 2024: Grades 3 to 6 have no face-to-face classes at Wind Signal No. 2 or higher.
- Only a flashlight is used in a power outage. The Student never holds a candle or matches.
- Windows are never taped or covered as the safe action. Stay away from windows, and keep them closed during the storm.
- Go with the grown-ups when the barangay or officials say to evacuate. Go out or go home only when officials say it is safe.
- Only Wind Signals No. 1, 2 and 3 appear. The hotline is 911.
- Floodwater, rivers, rainfall colour warnings and drinking water are left for the Flood Episode.

## Points to check

1. Hard 5 has weak sources. No primary source gives rules for children inside an evacuation center. Is "Look for your friend alone" acceptable, or should it change?
2. Hard 1, "Run back for your toy": no typhoon source names going back for a toy. It rests on "evacuate right away" (TY-51). Is that enough?
3. Hard 2 and Earthquake Normal 5 both end on high ground by the sea. Do they feel too alike? We do not know whether Baluan or the nearby coast is a storm surge area, so Hard 2 is a visit to Lola.
4. Hard 1 and Hard 2 happen before the typhoon arrives, in light rain, so nobody wears a raincoat. Should they?
5. Should any warning also appear in Filipino (for example "Bagyo" or "Signal No. 2" read aloud)? The plan keeps English only.
6. No General Santos CDRRMO hotline or real evacuation center was found, so the game uses 911 and a made-up evacuation center.
