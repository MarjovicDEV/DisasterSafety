# 01: Tracer: the Typhoon effect and Normal 1 classes suspended

**What to build:** The Learner opens Typhoon Normal 1 and plays it end to end. Typhoon Scenarios load from their own Episode folder, and Typhoon Normal 1 shows as a Playable Level. A Typhoon "during" Scenario does not shake. Instead heavy Rain, Wind and Dark Sky fade in over the `classroom` in about 1.5 seconds (rain streaming down the windows, grey panes, small leaves blowing past the top of the windows), the heavy rain and wind loops start under the music, the Teacher's speech bubble says "Signal No. 2. Classes are suspended." with the voice blip, the Student plays the surprised pose, and the Action Bar slides up. Pause stops the layers and the loops; Resume continues them. The three Choices are "Stay with the Teacher until Mom comes" (Safe: the Student walks to the Teacher and sits at the desk; Mom walks in holding her umbrella, and the Student walks to her), "Walk home alone" (the Student walks toward the door and stops inside the room) and "Wait outside at the gate" (the Student walks toward the door and stops before the threshold, facing out). Each Choice gets its own new `classroom` action key and icon (TY-12, TY-13, TY-32, TY-36). This ticket adds Mom's umbrella walk, the three recorded CC0 loops, the Typhoon content rules and the ADR 0005 rule test, so later tickets only add Levels. It also drafts the Captions, Hints, Feedback, lessons, Choice labels and Act-out lines of all five Normal Levels for the researcher.

**Blocked by:** None among the Typhoon tickets. Fire tickets 01 (the Episode-specific "during" effect) and 03 (per-Level layers on `LevelStart`) must be built first; read how they named the Fire effect property, the layer fields and the FI content rule, and name the Typhoon ones the same way.
