# 10: Hard 3 power out at night and Hard 4 the eye of the storm

**What to build:** Typhoon Hard 3 plays as a "during" Scenario in `home_night` with heavy Rain, Wind and the Night layer, and no warning bubble: the power is out, and a flashlight and an unlit candle with matches stand on the shelf. The Choices are "Turn on the flashlight and stay with Mom" (Safe: the Student walks to the shelf, picks up the flashlight, plays `flashlight` with the beam on, walks to Mom and sits beside her), "Light a candle" (the Student walks toward the shelf and stops before reaching it; no hand ever touches the candle or the matches), "Go out to check the lights" (the Student walks toward the door and stops) and "Play games on the phone" (the Student plays `phone` and stops) (TY-25, TY-27, TY-32, TY-39, TY-40, TY-44). Typhoon Hard 4 plays as a "during" Scenario in the `home` living room with heavy Rain, Wind and Dark Sky, and no warning bubble, then the calm step: after the Action Bar shows, the Rain and Wind fade out with their loops and the Dark Sky lightens. The Choices are "Stay inside and listen to the radio" (Safe: the Student walks to Mom and sits by the radio; the radio's bubble says "Stay inside."; the Rain and Wind fade back in with their loops), "Go out to play" (the Student run-starts toward the door and stops), "Get your bike from the yard" (the Student walks toward the door and stops; the bike is seen through the window) and "Visit your friend next door" (the Student walks toward the door, stops and faces the window) (TY-32, TY-35, TY-37, TY-46). This ticket adds the calm step, the bike, the phone, Typhoon's `home_night` and `home` action keys and one icon per Choice.

**Blocked by:** 03 (the `flashlight` pose) and 08 (the Hard text approved there). Fire tickets 05 (`home_night`) and 08 (the Night layer) must be built first.
