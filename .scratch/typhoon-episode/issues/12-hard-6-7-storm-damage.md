# 12: Hard 6 the fallen wire and Hard 7 the broken shed

**What to build:** Storm damage props on the start entry. Typhoon Hard 6 plays as an "after" Scenario on `street_storm`: a wire lies across the road with a branch on it, near a puddle, and Dad is nearby (the Tanod stands further off if room allows). The Choices are "Stay away and tell Dad" (Safe: the Student steps back from the wire, walks to Dad and calls "Dad, a wire!"; Dad plays `phone` to call 911), "Poke it with a stick" (the Student walks toward the stick, which lies far from the wire, plays `crouch` and stops before the wire), "Move the branch off the wire" (the Student walks toward the branch and stops far from it) and "Walk through the puddle by it" (the Student walks toward the puddle and stops before its edge) (TY-44, TY-45, TY-56, TY-65). Typhoon Hard 7 plays as an "after" Scenario in the `yard`: the shed is broken, and a roof sheet and broken glass lie on the grass, with a broom away from the glass; Dad is checking. The Choices are "Stay with Mom while Dad checks" (Safe: the Student walks to Mom by the house and stands with her; Dad looks over the shed), "Pick up the roof sheet" (the Student walks toward it and stops), "Go into the broken shed" (the Student walks toward the shed door and stops) and "Sweep up the broken glass" (the Student walks toward the broom and stops) (TY-55, TY-57, TY-59). This ticket adds the fallen branch, the fallen wire, the puddle, the stick, the roof sheet, the broken shed, the broken glass and the broom, Dad's `phone` pose (import `ArtSource/GrownUp/dad_phone.aseprite` unless a Fire ticket already has), Typhoon's `street_storm` and `yard` action keys and one icon per Choice.

**Blocked by:** 04 (Typhoon's `yard` support and Dad's `carry`) and 08 (`street_storm` and the Hard text).
