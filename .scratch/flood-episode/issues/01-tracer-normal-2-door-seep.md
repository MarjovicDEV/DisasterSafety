# 01: Tracer: the Flood effect and Normal 2 water under the door

**What to build:** The Learner opens Flood Normal 2 and plays it end to end. Flood Scenarios load from their own Episode folder, and Flood Normal 2 shows as a Playable Level. A Flood "during" Scenario does not shake. Instead, over `home` in about 1.5 seconds, Dark Sky and heavy Rain fade into the window, the window flood view shows low brown water over the yard, the heavy rain loop starts under the music, and the Floodwater seep under the closed front door spreads through its three steps and stays at step 3. Then the Student plays the surprised pose and the Action Bar slides up. No warning bubble plays; the Caption tells the situation. Pause stops the layers and the loop; Resume continues them. The three Choices are "Step back and tell Dad" (Safe: the Student walks right, away from the water, and calls "Dad, water!"; Dad walks in from the right edge to the switch box and turns off the main switch, and the stand fan stops), "Unplug the fan" (the Student walks toward the fan, plays `reach` and stops well short of the plug) and "Wipe up the water" (the Student walks toward the water, plays `crouch` and stops on dry floor). Each Choice gets its own new `home` action key and icon (FL-09, TY-43). This ticket adds the Floodwater layer on the per-Level start entry (the door seep and the window flood view), the stand fan and the low outlet, the Flood content rules and the Flood rule test, so later tickets only add Levels. It also drafts the Captions, Hints, Feedback, lessons, Choice labels and Act-out lines of all five Normal Levels for the user.

**Blocked by:** None among the Flood tickets. Art Batch 1 (Floodwater at home) must be approved and imported. Fire tickets 01 (the Episode-specific "during" effect), 03 (per-Level layers on `LevelStart`) and 07 (the switch box and `dad_switch`), and Typhoon tickets 01 (Rain, Dark Sky, the rain loops and the Typhoon effect property) and 03 (the `home` window views), must be built first. Read how they named the effect property, the layer fields and the per-Episode content rule, and name the Flood ones the same way.
