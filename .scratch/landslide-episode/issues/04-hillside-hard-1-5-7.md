# 04: `hillside` Hard 1 the Tanod's call, Hard 5 the cleanup, Hard 7 after the earthquake

**What to build:** Three Hard Levels on `hillside`, from art Batch 2, with the new `dad_shovel` pose. Hard 1 ("before", Rain and Dark Sky from the first frame): once the scene is still, the Barangay Tanod at the edge of the yard calls through the megaphone in a speech bubble ("Families by the hill, leave now!" or the approved wording; LS-16, TY-19, TY-20); then the Action Bar shows. Choices: "Leave now with Mom and Dad" (Safe: walk to Mom with the Go Bag and Dad at the house; the family walks off right behind the Tanod), "Wait for the rain to stop" (`sit` on the step), "Go back for your toy" (walk toward the open door, stop) and "Go look at the Slope" (walk toward the Slope, stop on the flat) (LS-17, LS-16, TY-19, TY-51). Hard 5 ("after", still, Fallen Soil, rope and sign; the Caption says the Tanod said the path may be cleared): Dad plays `dad_shovel` at the near edge of the soil. Choices: "Stay with Mom by the house" (Safe), "Grab a shovel and help", "Carry a pail of mud" (`crouch`) and "Play in the mud", each stopping on the flat at its own x (LS-28, TY-59; GAP). Hard 7 ("after", no Rain, nothing shakes; the Caption says the shaking stopped and the Student did "Duck, Cover, and Hold"): Mom and the Student start at the house door. Choices: "Go to the open yard, away from the Slope" (Safe: walk with Mom to the middle of the yard), "Wait by the Slope", "Sit under the coconut tree" and "Go up the path to look", each stopping before the Slope's foot or the path up it (LS-29, EQ-82). Each Choice gets its own `hillside` action key and icon.

**Blocked by:** 02 (the Hard text and Fallen Soil). Art Batch 2 must be approved and imported. The Typhoon ticket that adds the Barangay Tanod (its poses and the megaphone bubble) must be built first.
