# 05: `hill_road`: Normal 4 Muddy Runoff and Hard 2 the far slide

**What to build:** The new `hill_road` Setting (a road cut into a hill, with a far valley and a far bare hill), known and unable to shake, from art Batch 3. Normal 4 ("during"): Rain and Dark Sky fade in with Muddy Runoff running over the road from the cut Slope and a small heap of Fallen Soil at the road edge; then surprised and the Action Bar. Choices: "Go back the long way with Dad" (Safe: turn and walk back the way they came with Dad), "Cross the mud" and "Climb over the soil", each stopping before the runoff or the soil at its own x (LS-19, TY-52; GAP). Hard 2 ("during"): light Rain and Dark Sky fade in; the far slide on the far hill plays once (6 frames) and holds its last frame; the Tanod's line plays as a speech bubble; then surprised and the Action Bar. Choices: "Keep walking with the Tanod" (Safe: walk with Mom behind the Tanod to the EVACUATION ROUTE sign), "Take the Slope shortcut" (stop before the path's start), "Stop to watch the hill" (stop and turn to face the far hill) and "Run ahead alone" (run-start past the Tanod, stop) (LS-11, LS-19, TY-52, LS-02). This ticket adds Muddy Runoff and the far slide to the Landslide layers. Each Choice gets its own `hill_road` action key and icon.

**Blocked by:** 02 (the Hard text and Fallen Soil). The Normal text is from 01. Art Batch 3 (`hill_road`) must be approved and imported. The Typhoon ticket that adds the Barangay Tanod must be built first.

**Status:** ready-for-agent
