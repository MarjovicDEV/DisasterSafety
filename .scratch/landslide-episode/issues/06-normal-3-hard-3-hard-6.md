# 06: Normal 3 classroom, Hard 3 Evacuation Center, Hard 6 wall crack

**What to build:** Three Levels in reused Settings, from art Batch 4. Normal 3 (`classroom`, "during"): Rain and Dark Sky fade into the storm windows; the Teacher's warning plays as a speech bubble (classes stop and the hill road may slide; LS-18, TY-13, TY-14); then surprised and the Action Bar. Choices: "Wait with the Teacher for Mom" (Safe: walk to the Teacher, `sit` at a desk; Mom walks in with the umbrella and the Student walks to her), "Walk home by the hill road" (walk toward the door, stop inside) and "Wait outside at the gate" (stop before the threshold, facing out) (LS-18, LS-19). Hard 3 (`evacuation_area`, "after"; the Dark Sky clears; the friend is named in the Caption only): Choices: "Wait for the Tanod to say it is safe" (Safe: `sit` on the mat by Mom; the Tanod walks in and says through the megaphone that it is safe to go home), "Go home with your friend", "Go check your house" and "Run out to play", each stopping at its own x inside the court (LS-24, LS-23, TY-55). Hard 6 (`home`, "after", with the wall crack and the hill window view): Choices: "Tell Dad about the crack" (Safe: walk to Dad, `call` "Dad, a new crack!"; Dad turns toward the wall), "Hide it with a poster" (`carry` the poster toward the wall, stop), "Pick at the crack" (`reach`, stop short) and "Push the stuck door" (walk toward the front door, `reach`, stop short) (LS-07, LS-27). Each Choice gets its own action key on its Setting and an icon.

**Blocked by:** 02 (the Hard text). The Normal text is from 01. Art Batch 4 must be approved and imported. The Typhoon tickets that build the Normal 1 classroom with storm windows and the Evacuation Center Hard 5 layout with the Tanod, and the Flood ticket that adds the `home` window views, must be built first.
