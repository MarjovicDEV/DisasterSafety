# 02: Easy 1 the crack and Hard 4 the Fallen Soil

**What to build:** The rest of art Batch 1 on `hillside`. Landslide Easy 1 is a "before" Scenario that starts still with the new crack on the flat ground near the Slope's foot and shows the Action Bar after 1 second. Its two Choices are "Tell Dad about the crack" (Safe: the Student turns from the crack, walks to Dad by the house and calls "Dad, a crack!"; Dad turns toward the crack and stays well back from it) and "Jump over the crack" (run-start toward the crack, stop on the near side) (LS-04, LS-10; GAP: the child's action). Landslide Hard 4 is an "after" Scenario that starts still with Fallen Soil across the path at the Slope's foot, the rope and the LANDSLIDE ZONE sign, and a toy in the soil. Its four Choices are "Stay back with Mom" (Safe: walk back to Mom at the house door and stand with her), "Climb on the soil", "Get the toy from the soil" (`crouch`) and "Go close to look", each stopping before the rope at its own x (LS-25, LS-14, LS-23). This ticket adds Fallen Soil to the Landslide layers and the rope and sign props, and drafts the Captions, Hints, Feedback, lessons, Choice labels and Act-out lines of all three Easy Levels and all seven Hard Levels for the user.

**Blocked by:** 01 (the Landslide effect, layers and content rules). Art Batch 1 must be approved and imported.
