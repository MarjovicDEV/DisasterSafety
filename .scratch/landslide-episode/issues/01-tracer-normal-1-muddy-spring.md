# 01: Tracer: the Landslide effect, `hillside` and Normal 1 muddy spring

**What to build:** The Learner opens Landslide Normal 1 and plays it end to end. Landslide Scenarios load from their own Episode folder, and Landslide Normal 1 shows as a Playable Level. The new `hillside` Setting is known and cannot shake. A Landslide "during" Scenario does not shake. Instead, over `hillside` in about 1.5 seconds, Dark Sky and heavy Rain fade in, the heavy rain loop starts under the music, and the muddy spring at the foot of the Slope runs from a clear seep to muddy water and a small pool, then keeps its muddy loop. Then the Student plays the surprised pose and the Action Bar slides up. No warning bubble plays; the Caption tells the situation. Pause stops the layers and the loop; Resume continues them. The three Choices are "Go in and tell Mom" (Safe: the Student walks to the house door and calls "Mom, muddy water!"; Mom comes to the door), "Splash in the muddy water" (run-start toward the water, stop on the flat) and "Go up to look" (walk toward the spring, stop at a different x) (LS-06, LS-10). Each Choice gets its own `hillside` action key and icon. This ticket adds the Landslide effect property, the Landslide layers on the per-Level start entry (the Slide Sign layer with the spring; Fallen Soil and Muddy Runoff come in later tickets on the same seam), the Landslide content rules (LS IDs as known findings) and the Landslide rule test, so later tickets only add Levels. It also drafts the Captions, Hints, Feedback, lessons, Choice labels and Act-out lines of all five Normal Levels for the user.

**Blocked by:** None among the Landslide tickets. Art Batch 1 (`hillside` and its signs) must be approved and imported. Fire tickets 01 (the Episode-specific "during" effect) and 03 (per-Level layers on `LevelStart`), the Typhoon ticket that adds Rain, Dark Sky and the rain loops, and the Flood tracer (the Flood effect property, the Floodwater layer and the Flood rule test) must be built first. Read how they named the effect property, the layer fields and the per-Episode content rule, and name the Landslide ones the same way.

- `Scenario.Shakes` is true only for an Earthquake "during" Scenario. A Landslide "during" Scenario starts the Landslide effect, and Fire, Typhoon and Flood "during" Scenarios still start their own effects and not the Landslide one (EditMode tests for all five Episodes). `hillside` is a known Setting and is not in `CanShake`.
