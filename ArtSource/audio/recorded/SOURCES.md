# Recorded sound sources

Every file here is a recording made by a person (real objects and a real instrument), not synthesised or generated. All are CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and ship, with no credit required. `../prepare_recorded.py` turns them into the game's WAV files.
