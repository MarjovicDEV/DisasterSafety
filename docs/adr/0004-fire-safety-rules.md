# 0004: Fire safety rules where sources disagree

Date: 2026-11-26. Status: accepted for the Fire Episode.

## Context

The Fire research (docs/research/fire-safety.md) found that the primary sources disagree on several points that a Level must show one way. BFP is the main Philippine source; NFPA, USFA, the Red Cross and Ready.gov are the international sources. The Levels are in docs/design/fire-episode.md.

## Decision

- **Fire extinguisher:** the Student never uses one. A Grown-up may use one in the background. BFP's school guide keeps students out of any response (FI-46) and NFPA does not train children (FI-63). BFP's children's PASS song with adult supervision (FI-64) is not shown.
- **Stairs:** take the stairs, never the elevator (FI-45, FI-68). The BFP Volume 1 answer key that marks "Use the stairs" as NO is treated as an error.
- **Doors:** close doors behind you when you leave (FI-23), as NFPA and BFP's school guide say. BFP Volume 4's "leave the door open" for a kitchen fire is not used.
- **Wet cloth:** never put a wet cloth on a fire; only a Grown-up covers a pan with a lid (FI-51). The Student may hold a handkerchief over the nose while walking out, but it never slows the escape (FI-20).
- **Stop, Drop, and Roll:** BFP's wording, plus "Cover your face with your hands" (FI-25).
- **Burns:** "Cool the burn with cool water", with no time given.
- **Hotline:** always 911 (FI-77). No local fire station number was found.

## Consequences

No Level has a Choice where the Student uses a fire extinguisher, takes an elevator as the Safe Choice, or puts a wet cloth on a fire. S.A.F.E. may appear once in Feedback without its "Fight the small fire" step. The researcher reviews the Level list against these rules before the spec.

## Alternatives considered

Following BFP everywhere, as the Philippine authority, was rejected because BFP's own volumes disagree with each other on the extinguisher and the stairs, and its children's answer key contradicts every other source on the stairs.
