# Earthquake Safety for Grade 3-6 Learners (Philippines)

Date: 2026-11-16

Purpose: Source-backed earthquake safety facts for the game's Earthquake levels (first use: Easy / Level 1, classroom shaking). Every finding has a stable ID (EQ-xx) so game text can cite it.

## How this was gathered

- Primary sources only: PHIVOLCS (DOST), DepEd, NDRRMC, OCD.
- PHIVOLCS flyers and posters are linked from the PHIVOLCS "Earthquake Preparedness" page and hosted on Google Drive. They are image files. Quotes from them were read from the rendered image, not from extracted text. Spelling and capitals are kept as printed.
- DepEd PDFs were downloaded and their text was extracted. DO 53, s. 2022 is a scanned PDF with poor text recognition, so only clear lines are used.
- ocd.gov.ph and ndrrmc.gov.ph could not be fetched (HTTP 403, plus a bot check on ocd.gov.ph). No OCD or NDRRMC lines are quoted as primary. See the last section.

## 1. Official protective-action wording

- EQ-01: The PHIVOLCS-DepEd school primer uses "DUCK, COVER and HOLD" for classroom shaking. [S1] "Get under a sturdy table/desk and do the "DUCK, COVER and HOLD""
- EQ-02: The PHIVOLCS school drill primer uses "duck, cover and hold" for the drill response. [S2] "While the alarm is ongoing, everyone should perform "duck, cover and hold"."
- EQ-03: The PHIVOLCS public poster uses "DUCK, COVER and HOLD". [S3] "Do the "DUCK, COVER and HOLD"." The Filipino poster keeps the English phrase. [S4] "Gawin ang "DUCK, COVER and HOLD"."
- EQ-04: PHIVOLCS NSED news uses "Duck, Cover, and Hold". [S7] "prompts everyone to do the Duck, Cover, and Hold" and [S8] "performed the Duck, Cover, and Hold technique and evacuated the building"
- EQ-05: Recent PHIVOLCS earthquake primers use "Drop, Cover, and Hold" instead. [S5] (2026) "the public is advised to protect themselves by doing the "Drop, Cover, and Hold" procedure." [S6] (2019) "doing the "drop, cover and hold" if inside a structurally sound building."
- EQ-06: DepEd Order No. 27, s. 2015 uses "Duck-Cover-Hold" and also "Duck-Hold-Cover (DCH)". [S9] "the initial response during an earthquake (Duck-Cover-Hold)?" and "Duck-Hold-Cover (DCH) as an initial response during earthquakes"
- EQ-07: A DepEd Grade 10 activity sheet uses a fourth form. [S11] "Earthquake: Drop, Under, Cover, Hold On"
- EQ-08: DepEd regional NSED reporting (2026) uses "Duck, Cover, and Hold". [S12] "followed the standard safety protocol, "Duck, Cover, and Hold,""
- EQ-09: Recommended game wording: **"Duck, Cover, and Hold"**. Reasons: (a) it is the term in both school-specific PHIVOLCS primers made with or for DepEd [S1][S2]; (b) DepEd's own order uses it [S9]; (c) it is the term used in NSED drills that Grade 3-6 learners already practice [S8][S12]. "Drop, Cover, and Hold" in recent PHIVOLCS primers [S5] means the same action. The game could show it once as an alternate name, for example in a teacher or parent note. Avoid "Duck-Hold-Cover" and "Drop, Under, Cover, Hold On". Each appears in one document only.

## 2. What a learner does in the classroom during shaking

- EQ-10: Get under a sturdy table or desk and stay there until the shaking stops. [S1] "Get under a sturdy table/desk ... Stay put until the shaking stops."
- EQ-11: Hold on to the desk or table. If you cannot, protect your head with your arms. [S3] "Duck under a sturdy desk or table, and hold on to it, or protect your head with your arms."
- EQ-12: Hold on to your cover until the shaking stops. [S2] (picture caption) "Take cover under a sturdy table and hold to your cover until the shaking stops"
- EQ-13: Stay in the position until the shaking stops. [S2] "Remain in this position until the "shaking" stops."
- EQ-14: Stay away from glass windows, shelves, cabinets and heavy objects. [S3] "Stay away from glass windows, shelves, cabinets and other heavy objects." [S2] "Keep away from glass window and heavy shelves"
- EQ-15: Watch out for falling objects. [S2] "Watch out for falling objects" [S3] "Beware of falling objects. Be alert and keep your eyes open."
- EQ-16: Inside a structurally sound building, stay inside. [S3] "When you are INSIDE a structurally sound building or home...STAY THERE!"
- EQ-17: A door can be opened for the exit, by an assigned learner in drills. [S3] "If possible quickly open the door for exit." [S2] "Assigned student in-charge of making sure the door is open during shaking"

## 3. Why the wrong choices are unsafe

### Running outside

- EQ-18: PHIVOLCS says to stay inside a sound building during shaking, not run out. [S3] "When you are INSIDE a structurally sound building or home...STAY THERE!"
- EQ-19: Learners move only after the shaking stops. [S1] "As soon as the shaking stops... Leave the classroom immediately." [S2] "As soon as the shaking stops, immediately evacuate the school building"
- EQ-20: Running is not allowed, even during evacuation. [S1] (instructions to students) "WALK. DO NOT RUN. DO NOT PUSH. DO NOT TALK."
- Note: No primary source found that gives the reason in words (for example, falling while running, or debris at exits). See "Unsourced or uncertain".

### Standing by the window

- EQ-21: Windows and glass panes are listed as unsafe zones in school. [S2] "Some of the unsafe zones: Windows and glass panes"
- EQ-22: Broken window glass is a falling-object danger. [S1] "Stay away from falling objects such as pieces of broken glass windows, ceiling fans, etc."
- EQ-23: Strong shaking can injure people through broken glass. [S1] "Strong ground shaking can cause injuries to people due to broken glasses and fallen objects."
- EQ-24: Window panes may break during shaking. [S1] "window panes may rattle and may even break."

### Panicking, crying, freezing

- EQ-25: Stay calm. [S3] "STAY CALM." [S4] "MAGING MAHINAHON." [S2] "Keep calm and don't panic"
- EQ-26: Panic stops clear thinking. [S9] "Panic could result to inability to think during emergencies"
- EQ-27: Do not panic after the shaking either. [S3] (under "AFTER ... Don't...") "...PANIC."
- EQ-28: Protect yourself first. [S1] "When a strong shaking starts... Protect yourself."
- Note: "Cry and stay still" is partly wrong, not fully wrong. Staying still in an open spot does not protect the head from falling objects (EQ-15, EQ-22). No primary source mentions crying or freezing by name.

## 4. Falling-object hazards in classrooms and schools

- EQ-29: Falling objects to avoid include broken window glass and ceiling fans. [S1] "Stay away from falling objects such as pieces of broken glass windows, ceiling fans, etc."
- EQ-30: School hazard list from the PHIVOLCS-DepEd primer. [S1]
  - "Large and heavy pieces of furniture that could topple, such as book cases and cabinets;"
  - "Hanging flower pots and ceiling fans;"
  - "Glass window panes and walls that could break;"
  - "Heavy picture frames and mirrors;"
  - "Latches on cabinets that will not hold cabinet door closed;"
  - "Glass or other breakable objects that are kept on high and open shelves;"
  - "Obstructions such as plant boxes, chairs and benches, bulletin boards, trash cans that may block exit points and corridors; and"
  - "Elevated water tanks and electrical posts."
- EQ-31: Things that move or fall during shaking. [S1] "hanging objects like lamps, wall frames swing violently or may even fall down." and "bookshelves, cabinets, tables and chairs may move, be shifted and/or fall over."
- EQ-32: Unsafe zones in school include shelves and furniture that can topple. [S2] "Book shelves, machinery, cabinets and furniture that may topple or slide"
- EQ-33: Hanging objects should be fastened beforehand. [S5] "hanging objects properly fastened to prevent injuries." [S3] "Check the stability of hanging objects like ceiling fans and chandeliers."

## 5. After the shaking stops

- EQ-34: Leave the classroom and building in an orderly way. [S1] "Leave the classroom immediately. Get out of the building in an orderly manner."
- EQ-35: Follow the class marshal or teacher on the set route. [S2] "evacuate the school building and proceed to identified evacuation areas using the pre-determined routes guided by the class marshall or teacher."
- EQ-36: Walk. Do not run, push, or talk. [S1] "WALK. DO NOT RUN. DO NOT PUSH. DO NOT TALK."
- EQ-37: Go to the evacuation area. [S1] "Proceed to the identified evacuation area."
- EQ-38: The evacuation area is an open space near the building. [S2] "Use all available OPEN SPACES nearest to the building"
- EQ-39: At the evacuation area, group by class and teachers count learners. [S2] "students must be grouped together according to the class where they belong." and "Teachers should check and make sure all students are accounted for."
- EQ-40: Wait for the "all clear". [S2] "The over-all coordinator will annouce the termination of drill or "All clear"."
- EQ-41: When to evacuate. [S2] "After a very strong earthquake wherein you lose balance or could not stand, and heavy objects and furniture start to be moved or shifted." and "As need arises or as declared by authorities."
- EQ-42: Expect aftershocks. [S3] "Be prepared for aftershocks." [S5] "We can expect aftershocks in the epicentral area."
- EQ-43: Do not go back into damaged buildings. [S3] "Don't... ...enter damaged buildings." [S9] "we should not go back to our homes/buildings due to possible effects of aftershocks?"
- EQ-44: Outside, stay away from trees, power lines, posts and concrete structures. [S3] "Stay away from trees, powerlines, posts and concrete structures."
- EQ-45: Covering the head while walking out: the [S2] drill primer shows (Phase 3 picture) learners walking out with books held over their heads. This is a picture only. No text says "duck, cover, hold while walking". Treat as uncertain (see last section).
- EQ-46: Schools must hold drills often. [S10] "all public schools are hereby required to conduct unannounced earthquake and fire drills every first and third week of every month." (Clean reading of a scanned page. Words were checked by eye against the garbled text.)

## 6. If no desk is available

- EQ-47: DepEd says Duck, Cover, Hold must also be practiced where there is no table. [S9] "should be practiced in different contexts, especially in areas without tables or any stable furniture to stay under."
- EQ-48: If you cannot hold on to a desk, protect your head with your arms. [S3] "...hold on to it, or protect your head with your arms."
- EQ-49: Use a book to cover your head. [S2] (picture caption) "Use a book to cover your head"
- EQ-50: Hide under an armchair (school armchair desk). [S2] (picture caption) "Hide under an armchair"
- EQ-51: Still stay away from windows, shelves and heavy objects. [S3] "Stay away from glass windows, shelves, cabinets and other heavy objects."

## Game mapping (Easy / Level 1)

- "Hide under the desk and hold on": correct. EQ-01, EQ-10, EQ-11, EQ-13.
- "Run outside": wrong. EQ-16, EQ-19, EQ-20.
- "Stand by the window": wrong. EQ-14, EQ-21, EQ-22.
- "Cry and stay still": wrong. EQ-25, EQ-26, EQ-28, EQ-15.
