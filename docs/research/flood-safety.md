# Flood Safety for Grade 3-6 Learners (Philippines)

Date: 2026-11-27

Purpose: Source-backed flood safety facts for the game's Flood Episode (Episode 4). Every finding has a stable ID (FL-xx) so game text can cite it. Typhoon findings that also cover floods (TY-08, TY-09, TY-14, TY-47 to TY-49, TY-52, TY-58, TY-60) stay in docs/research/typhoon-safety.md and are cited from here by their TY IDs.

## How this was gathered

- PAGASA pages were read live in a browser on 2026-11-27. The "Flood Safety Rules" and "Countermeasures and Mitigations of Flood Damage" tabs of [S1] were read word for word from the page text.
- doh.gov.ph (national DOH) showed a bot check ("Performing security verification") and HTTP 403. The check was not bypassed. No national DOH page was read. Two pages of the DOH Bicol Center for Health Development (an official regional DOH office) were read instead [S3] [S4].
- www1.pagasa.dost.gov.ph (the older PAGASA hydromet pages) did not resolve (DNS failure). Not read.
- Philippine Government works are quoted (RA 8293, Sec. 176). Quotes keep the pages' own spelling, with [sic] where it is wrong.

## 1. Warnings

- FL-01: PAGASA flood bulletins name the danger in steps. [S1] "A "Flood Advisory" is a warning that states the imminence of a flood situation." "A "Flood Warning" is issued when a flooding situation is a definite reality at least 24 hours before actual flooding occurs." [S2] river levels: "Flood Alert" ("Advised to be alert for possible flood, flash flood and landslides"), "Flood Warning" ("Advised to take appropriate action"), "Severe Flood Warning" ("Advised to force evacuation").
  - Unsafe choice: ignoring a flood warning on the radio because the street is still dry.
- FL-02: PAGASA rainfall warnings. [S2] Advisory: "Flooding is POSSIBLE in low-lying areas and near river channels." Alert: "Flooding is THREATHENING [sic] in low-lying areas and near river channels." Emergency: "SEVERE Flooding is EXPECTED. Take necessary precautionary measures." The colours (yellow, orange, red) and the actions per colour are in TY-08 and TY-09.
- FL-03: Know the warning system and listen to the radio. [S1] "Know the flood warning system in your community and be sure your family knows it." "Keep informed of daily weather condition." "Listen to your radio for emergency instructions."
  - Unsafe choice: turning off the radio during heavy rain.

## 2. Before the flood

- FL-04: Put things above the expected water. [S1] "Store supplies and other household effects above expected flood water level." "Move household belongings to upper levels." "Electrically operated appliances should be transferred to upper storeys of buildings." A child moves light things only (game rule, as TY-28).
  - Unsafe choice: leaving the school bag and books on the floor when the rain does not stop.
- FL-05: Do not throw garbage into canals and rivers. [S1] "Do not throw garbage in esteros and rivers." "Avoid throwing anything like plastic wrappers anywhere which may clog or block the drainage system." "Help clean the neighborhood." [S3] "Ensure proper disposal of waste".
  - Unsafe choice: dropping a snack wrapper into the canal.
- FL-06: Plan where the family goes. [S1] "Designate an evacuation area for the family and livestock." "Assign family members instructions and responsibilities according to an evacuation plan."
- FL-07: Keep supplies ready. [S1] "Keep a stock of food which requires little cooking and refrigeration; electric power may be interrupted." "Keep a transistorized radio and flashlight with spare batteries, emergency cooking equipment, candies [sic], matches and first aid kit handy in case of emergency." "Store drinking water in containers, water service may be interrupted." The matches follow ADR 0005: the game shows a flashlight and never a child with matches.

## 3. When warned, and during the flood

- FL-08: Leave before the water cuts the way. [S1] "Watch for rapidly rising flood waters." "If you find it necessary to evacuate, move to a safe area before access is cut off by flood waters." "Where houses are expected to be flooded, people should move to higher places." [S2] Severe Flood Warning: "Flood is persisting force evacuation is recommended". See also TY-09 (red: "Evacuate to higher ground").
  - Unsafe choice: waiting until the water comes into the house before leaving.
- FL-09: Power off when water reaches the outlets (a Grown-up's job). [S1] "When electrical fines [sic] and outlets will be submerged in floodwater, power should be switched off." "Turn off electricity at the main switch in the building before evacuating and also lock your house."
  - Unsafe choice: a child pulling a plug or touching a switch near the water. (No Philippine source names this; Ready.gov does, TY-43.)
- FL-10: Children stay out of floodwater. [S1] "People, particularly children, should avoid wading in floodwaters." [S3] "Avoid swimming and submerging in contaminated water or flood water" and "Use boots and gloves when coming into contact with contaminated water or flood water".
  - Unsafe choice: wading in to get a ball, or splashing in the flooded yard.
- FL-11: Do not cross moving water, flooded roads or bridges. [S1] "Do not attempt to cross rivers of flowing streams where water is above the knee." "Beware of water-covered roads and bridges." See also TY-48 and TY-52.
  - Unsafe choice: crossing the flooded road to get home faster.
- FL-12: Stay away from rivers and places that flood fast. [S1] "Avoid areas subject to sudden flooding." "Do not go swimming or boating in swollen rivers." "Avoid unnecessary exposure to the elements." [S2] Flood Alert: "Alert for possible flash floods and landslides".
  - Unsafe choice: going to the riverbank or the bridge to watch the big river.
- FL-13: Drink only clean or boiled water, eat only cooked food. [S1] "Eat only well-cooked food. Protect leftovers against contamination. Drink clean or preferably boiled water ONLY." [S3] "Drink only clean water". See also TY-58 ("boiled water", no time).
  - Unsafe choice: drinking rainwater from the drum.

## 4. After the flood

- FL-14: Do not eat food or drink water that floodwater may have touched until it is checked. [S1] "Do not eat food and drink water until they have been checked for flood water contamination."
  - Unsafe choice: eating bread from the cupboard the water reached.
- FL-15: Power stays off until an electrician checks. [S1] "Do not turn on the main switch or use appliances and other equipment until they have been checked by a competent electrician." "Be alert for fire hazards like broken wires." "Report broken utility lines (electricity, water, gas and telephone) to appropriate agencies authorities."
  - Unsafe choice: turning on the TV or plugging in the fan on the wet floor.
- FL-16: Go back in with a flashlight. [S1] "Re-enter the dwellings with caution using flashlights, not lanterns or torchers [sic]. Flammables may be inside."
- FL-17: Do not go to see the flooded places. [S1] "Do not go in disaster areas. Your presence might hamper rescue and other emergency operations."
  - Unsafe choice: going with friends to look at the flooded street.
- FL-18: Wash skin that touched floodwater. [S3] "Wash with clean water the exposed areas of body to contaminated water or flood water" and "consult your nearest primary care provider as soon as symptoms of leptospirosis arise."
  - Unsafe choice: wiping wet feet on clothes and going on playing.
- FL-19: Stop mosquitoes breeding in rainwater. [S4] "Search and destroy mosquito breeding places – cover water-holding containers" and "Self-protection – wear long-sleeved shirts and long pants". [S3] "Maintain cleanliness of house and surroundings". See also TY-60.
  - Unsafe choice: leaving a can or an old tire full of rainwater in the yard.
- FL-20: Health checks after the flood. [S1] "Consult health authorities for immunization requirements." (A Grown-up's job; the game does not show it.)

## Unsourced or uncertain

- National DOH pages (doh.gov.ph) were blocked: the advice on soap, on seeing a doctor after any wading, on boiling time, and the "W.I.L.D." diseases message were seen only in search snippets. SECONDARY ONLY.
- No Philippine source read says a child must not touch plugs or switches near water (FL-09). Only Ready.gov (TY-43, US).
- PAGASA [S1] says "Drink clean or preferably boiled water ONLY" during the flood, but names checking tap water only after the flood (FL-14). Whether tap water is unsafe during the flood is not settled.
- No source read says a river can keep rising after the local rain stops. [S2] warns of flash floods with heavy rainfall only.
- Whether Barangay Baluan or General Santos floods: one news report (MindaNews, July 2026, in typhoon-safety.md) only. SECONDARY ONLY.
- Snakes and animals in floodwater: only NOAA (TY-49, US). Not used.
- NDRRMC and OCD pages were not read (HTTP 403 in the typhoon research). OCD flood tips were seen only in news. SECONDARY ONLY.
